Revive / logo reset / 2026

Three fresh
logo directions.

A blank-slate exploration for a fintech brand that needs to feel financially capable, trustworthy and unmistakably modern. Each route starts with the word revive; the references are cues for tone and craft, not templates to copy.

Clear by nature.

Direct, open and human. Strong lettering and a compact exchange mark make the financial promise easy to recognise.
